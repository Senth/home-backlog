# ux-review

**Verdict:** FAIL
**Viewports:** 2560x1440, 390x844 (plus 360, 320, 260 and 195 CSS px as 100 %/150 %/200 % browser zoom on a phone)
**Screens seen:** splash (cold boot, CPU-throttled), login (light, dark, offline, error snackbar, busy attempt, `en-US` + `sv-SE`), Projects empty state (initials avatar and photo avatar), Locations, Maintenance, account menu (mobile, desktop, dark, narrow), sign-out confirmation dialog (mobile, desktop, dark, `sv-SE`, 200 % zoom), offline bar signed in and signed out, forced Google account chooser, signed-in deep-link reload.

The feature's headline promises hold. A cold load of `/` never mounts a route before auth resolves — the path stayed `/` through five throttled frames and then went straight to `/login`; a throttled reload of `/projects` while signed in showed the splash and then Projects, with no login flash. `prompt: 'select_account'` really does force the chooser on every sign-in (I switched Marcus → Anna → Marcus without clearing anything). The empty-state "Signed in as anna@example.com" line changed with the account and is the thing that would actually catch a wrong-account sign-in on a phone. Both avatar branches render, the photo one survives going offline, and the offline bar swaps its wording correctly between `status.offline` and `status.offlineSignedOut` in both locales. Dark mode is clean everywhere I looked (title 9.45:1, button 9.04:1, dialog actions 8.25:1). What fails is craft on the two controls that matter most: the sign-out dialog and the single sign-in button.

## Findings

1. **[blocking]** The sign-out confirmation dialog does not trap focus, cannot be closed with Escape, and does not restore focus when it closes. Opening it from the menu leaves `document.activeElement` on the app-bar avatar; the first Tab goes to the *Projects* tab link behind the scrim, then Locations, Maintenance, and only then "Close modal", Cancel, Sign out. Escape does nothing even with focus inside the dialog. Dismissing via the backdrop drops focus on `<body>`. — *craft: focus and keyboard* — `shots/ux-2560x1440-signout-dialog.png`, `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog.png`
   Fix: wrap the Paper `Dialog` in a focus trap (move focus to the dialog on open, cycle Tab within it, `onDismiss` on Escape) and return focus to the `AccountMenu` trigger on close.
2. **[blocking]** The dialog's two actions fail contrast in the light theme: `colors.primary` `#2E7D32` on the dialog surface `#EEE8F4` is **4.27:1** at 14 px / weight 500 — under the 4.5:1 body-text threshold. It is the same for "Avbryt" / "Logga ut". Dark mode is fine (8.25:1). — *craft: colour* — `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog.png`, `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog-sv.png`
   Fix: darken the light-theme `primary` (or give dialog actions `onSurface`/`error` roles) until every 14 px label clears 4.5:1.
3. **[blocking]** The only control on the login screen truncates under zoom. At 260 CSS px (≈150 % zoom on a 390 px phone) the button reads "Continue …"; at 195 px (200 %) it reads "C…" — label `scrollWidth` 136 against `clientWidth` 82, `text-overflow: ellipsis`, one line. §5 states the Paper `Button` label wraps rather than truncating; it does not. The screen does scroll (content 504 px in a 422 px viewport), so only the label is at fault. — *craft: `sv-SE`/text scaling, and Ingrid* — `shots/ux-260x560-login-zoom150.png`, `shots/ux-195x422-login-zoom200-scrolled.png`
   Fix: let the button label wrap (`numberOfLines={0}` / no ellipsis) and stretch the button to the card width so the label has the full clamp to wrap into.
4. **[blocking]** Touch targets on the two consequential controls sit under the project's own `touchTarget = 48`. "Continue with Google" is **40 dp** tall at 390x844 (and at 2560); the dialog's "Cancel" is 60x**38** and "Sign out" 68x**38**, with an 8 px gap between them — two sub-target hit areas next to each other, one of which is the lockout. The comment in `theme/tokens.ts` claims "Paper's own controls already honour it"; Paper's `Button` does not. — *craft: touch targets* — `shots/ux-390x844-login.png`, `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog.png`
   Fix: give the login `Button` and both dialog actions `contentStyle={{ minHeight: touchTarget }}`, and widen the gap between Cancel and Sign out to `space.md`.
5. **[should-fix]** The app mark is a network-fetched asset with no fallback, so the two screens whose whole job is "this is not broken" render a hole. Offline on the login screen there is no `<img>` and no background image at all — an empty 96 px gap above the title — and it does **not** come back when the connection returns without a reload (`ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED` on `assets/images/icon.png`). On a CPU-throttled cold boot the splash is a blank surface with a small green spinner and zero images in the DOM for ~1 s: exactly the "blank surface (indistinguishable from a white-screen crash)" §2 rejected. — *craft: states* — `shots/ux-390x844-login-offline.png`, `shots/splash-3.png`
   Fix: inline the mark (SVG or data URI) or precache it in the service worker, and render a `primaryContainer` placeholder of `size.brandMark` while it loads. (Seen against the dev bundle — worth confirming against the production precache before deciding how far to go.)
6. **[should-fix]** At 390x844 the sign-out dialog spans the full screen width — measured `x: 0, width: 390` — so its 28 px corners sit flush against both screen edges. Paper's own `marginHorizontal: 26` is computed but overridden, presumably by the `contentWidth.dialog` clamp; at 195 px the margins reappear, so it is width-dependent rather than deliberate. — *craft: tokens and Material 3* — `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog.png` vs `shots/ux-195x422-signout-dialog.png`
   Fix: clamp with `maxWidth: contentWidth.dialog` and `alignSelf: 'center'` instead of a width that cancels the inset.
7. **[should-fix]** The account menu is a fixed 218 px wide and overflows the *left* edge at 200 % zoom: at a 195 px viewport it lands at `x: -26`, clipping the header avatar in half and cutting the leading `logout` icon off "Logga ut". At 320 px it clears by 2 px. — *craft: responsiveness, and Ingrid* — `shots/ux-195x640-accountmenu.png`, `shots/ux-320x640-accountmenu.png`
   Fix: constrain the menu to the viewport (`maxWidth` from the window width minus `space.md` on each side) so it shrinks instead of sliding off.
8. **[should-fix]** In Swedish the offline login screen says the same sentence twice within 550 px: the bar reads "Offline — du måste vara uppkopplad för att logga in." and the hint under the button "Du måste vara uppkopplad för att logga in." Word for word, one above the other. The `en-US` pair differs enough ("Offline — you need a connection to sign in." / "Signing in needs a connection.") to read as two facts; the Swedish reads as a stutter. — *craft: `sv-SE`, and Ingrid* — `shots/ux-390x844-login-offline-sv.png`
   Fix: reword `screen.login.offlineHint` in `sv-SE` to say something the bar does not, e.g. "Knappen fungerar igen när du är uppkopplad."
9. **[should-fix]** In the dialog, the action that logs you out is styled identically to the one that does not: same `primary` green, same weight, same size, 8 px apart, with the destructive one on the right where a thumb rests. §2 justifies this dialog as the guard against a 71-year-old losing all access; the guard currently offers two identical-looking answers. — *Ingrid* — `shots/ux-390x844-signout-dialog-sv.png`
   Fix: give the confirm `textColor={colors.error}` (or make Cancel the emphasised `contained` action) so the two are never mistaken at a glance.
10. **[idea]** At 2560x1440 the signed-in screen is the phone layout stretched: a full-width bottom tab bar with three items 800 px apart, the title alone at the far left, the account chip 2400 px away at the far right, and a 12 px "Signed in as…" line marooned in the middle. The tab screens are placeholders by design, so this is not this feature's debt — but the account chip and the empty-state footnote are, and both are hard to notice at that width. — *craft: responsiveness, and Marcus* — `shots/ux-2560x1440-projects.png`
    Fix: when the boards land, constrain the signed-in content to a max width and consider a rail instead of a bottom bar above `compactBreakpoint`.
11. **[idea]** The error snackbar carries no action. `common.retry` ("Try again" / "Försök igen") and `common.dismiss` already exist in both locales and neither is used; the two-line Swedish message auto-hides on Paper's timer whether or not it has been read. — *craft: states* — `shots/ux-390x844-snackbar-sv.png`
    Fix: give the snackbar a `common.retry` action that re-runs the sign-in, or at minimum `common.dismiss`.
12. **[idea]** The splash's `accessibilityLabel` is announced as "Loading" for the first few frames and only becomes "Laddar" once i18n resolves — a Swedish screen-reader user hears one English word per cold start.
    Fix: resolve the locale before the first splash render, or leave the splash unlabelled until it can be labelled correctly.

## Persona pass

### Marcus — the renovator
He signs in on the laptop, gets his name beside the avatar in the app bar, and never thinks about auth again — which is the correct outcome. The thing he would genuinely have hit is the work-versus-personal Google account, and the forced chooser handles it: I signed in as Marcus, switched to Anna and back without clearing a cookie, and the empty state relabelled itself each time. His one live irritation is finding 400 px of content on a 2560 px monitor (finding 10) — after a decade of Trello he expects a desktop app to use the desktop. Nothing here erodes nesting, the location tree or the stages; the feature touches none of them.

### Nadia — the half-engaged partner
She is in the app because Marcus is, on an Android phone, and she will not go looking for anything. The account menu is the only tappable thing in the app bar, and everything inside it is either information she did not ask for or the button that removes her from the household board. If she taps the avatar out of curiosity — the one exploratory tap this persona ever makes — the single offered action is "Logga ut". That is a different problem from Ingrid's (finding 9 is about mis-tapping the confirm; this is about the menu's only affordance being destructive), and it resolves itself when "Switch home" arrives in #21. Worth remembering that the menu currently reads as a sign-out drawer no matter what §5 intends it to become.

### Ingrid — the non-technical owner
October, the chimney sweep has just left, and she opens the app on her iPhone with the text size turned up. At the zoom that gives her, the sign-in button reads "C…" (finding 3) — the only control on the screen, with nothing but a Google "G" to explain it, and she does not distinguish an app from a website so she has no mental model to fill the gap. If she gets in, the account menu she might open by accident hangs 26 px off the left edge with half an avatar showing (finding 7), and the confirmation that is supposed to protect her offers two 38 dp green words 8 px apart (findings 4 and 9). The Swedish itself is good — "Inloggad som", "Du behöver logga in med Google igen", "Inga projekt än" all read like Swedish, not translated English — except the doubled offline sentence (finding 8). One thing no fix reaches: the title on the screen she stares at while signing in is "Home Backlog", and "backlog" is precisely her wall; the tagline underneath is the only Swedish thing there. That is a settled naming decision, noted below.

### Tom — the seasonal maintainer
He is in the garage with gloves on and no signal, and this feature treats him honestly: the bar says changes are saved and will sync, the board stays, the avatar photo survives from cache. The failure that would reach him is finding 5 — if his session ever does drop out there (an old iPhone, a cleared browser, a reinstall), what he gets is a card with an empty grey rectangle where the logo belongs, a dead button, and a line telling him he needs a connection he does not have. A missing logo is what a broken app looks like to someone who is unenthusiastic about the phone in the first place, and once the icon request has failed it never recovers even after he walks back into signal — he has to know to reload. Everything else here he would not notice, which is the right result for him.

### Priya — the `en-US` check
The `en-US` copy is clean and nothing about the sign-in path is visibly Nordic — no seasons, no units, no assumptions. Her distinct exposure is the one §8 documents and does not mitigate: on plain iOS Safari the session evicts after about a week idle, and she uses the app in twenty-minute gaps between kids. When that happens she is dropped on a login screen that says nothing about why, and "it logged me out again" is exactly the friction that ends an app for someone with no patience. Not a finding against this change — no web app can prevent the eviction — but if an install nudge ever returns, the moment right after an unexplained sign-out is where it earns its place.

### Kasper — the agent-driver
*Nothing to add for this feature* — web session UI, no API surface touched, and API keys in the account menu are explicitly deferred to #7.

## Open questions

1. The in-flight state of the sign-in button (§5: `loading` + `disabled` from the tap until the browser navigates away, so a double tap cannot start two redirects) was not captured. Blocking or delaying the emulator's `/emulator/auth/handler` navigation either aborts to a Chrome error page or wedges the session, and the un-delayed window is a few hundred milliseconds. Marcus double-tapping a slow button is the case this protects; someone should confirm it visually or in code.
2. Whether the brand mark in finding 5 is precached by the production service worker. Everything I saw was the dev bundle, where `assets/images/icon.png` is fetched over the network; if the built PWA precaches it the offline hole shrinks to "first load only", which changes the fix, not the finding.

## Out of scope / already decided

- A second sign-in method as the answer to Ingrid's lockout risk — **Google sign-in only**, PROJECT.md §*Platform, auth, i18n*; the spec answers it with the confirmation dialog instead, and Sign in with Apple stays a pre-launch task.
- Translating or softening the "Home Backlog" title on the login screen for Ingrid — the product name is settled in PROJECT.md §*Naming*.
